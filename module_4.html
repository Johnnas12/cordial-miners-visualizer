Supporting weight / total active weight

This is one weighted quorum condition used in consensus reasoning. Passing it alone does not finalize a leader or emit tau order.

These weights are illustrative, not the output of the PoR rating formula. A new weight snapshot activates only at a finalized checkpoint; it cannot rewrite a decision already made. In the penalty example, D has zero voting weight, but that alone does not change validator membership or the leader schedule. The sample never assigns faulty D one-third of active weight.
